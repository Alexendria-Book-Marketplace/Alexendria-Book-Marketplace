Here's How Shipping Works

Before buying a book, confirm the shipping cost, delivery method, and estimated arrival time with the seller.

Ask the seller how the book will be packaged and whether tracking is available. Check that your delivery details are correct before agreeing to the purchase.

👥

How We Connect You

Alexandria connects people who want to buy and sell used books and textbooks. Buyers can browse by genre and price, and sellers can list books they no longer need.

Compare book titles, editions, prices, and condition before choosing a listing. If you are selling, include a clear description so buyers know what they are getting.

🔒

How to Stay Secure

Use a strong password and keep your login details private. Check the book description and seller details before buying. Never share your password or verification codes.

Be cautious if someone asks for your password or sends an unfamiliar link. If an offer seems suspicious, stop and check the details before continuing.
