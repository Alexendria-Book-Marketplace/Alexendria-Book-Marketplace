How We Connect You

Alexandria connects people who want to buy and sell used books and textbooks. Buyers can browse by genre and price, and sellers can list books they no longer need.

How to Stay Secure

Use a strong password and keep your login details private. Check the book description and seller details before buying. Never share your password or verification codes.
